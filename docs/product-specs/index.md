# Product Specs

Product specs describe user-visible behavior: what a learner can do, why it matters, and how a human can recognize the behavior in the running app. They should avoid implementation details unless those details shape the product experience.

Current specs:

- `product-vision.md` — Qwizle's product goal, current slice, and explicit non-goals.
- `daily-learning-challenges.md` — the first daily learning challenge experience and future boundaries.

Use an ExecPlan in `docs/exec-plans/` when a spec becomes a complex feature or significant refactor.
